Privacy Policy

shorts-mill desktop client is used only by its owner to upload videos to, and read video status from, the owner's own YouTube channels via the YouTube Data API.

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer.
  • No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
